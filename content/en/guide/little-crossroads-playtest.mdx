## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get selected for the little crossroads playtest?
Access is managed through the Steam Playtest widget on the game's official Steam store page. Simply log in, click "Request Access," and add the game to your wishlist. When Red Merle Games opens new participant slots, Steam will automatically send you an invitation email granting access to install the test build.

### Will progress from the little crossroads playtest carry over to full launch?
No. Because the game is in active early development ahead of its planned 2027 release, playtest data and character saves are subject to resets between major test builds and will not transfer to the final commercial release.

### Can I play the Little Crossroads playtest entirely solo?
Yes. While the magical signpost system allows real-time multiplayer visits with friends, you can completely ignore multiplayer and enjoy the game as a relaxing, standalone single-player life simulator.

### Is the playtest free of charge?
Yes. Participating in the playtest via Steam is completely free. You do not need to pre-order or purchase the game to test development builds when invites are sent out.
