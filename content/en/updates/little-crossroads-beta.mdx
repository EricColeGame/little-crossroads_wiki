## What Is Little Crossroads? A New Wave of Cozy Multiplayer Gaming

*Little Crossroads* is an upcoming multiplayer cozy RPG developed and published by independent studio Red Merle Games. Designed as a sanctuary away from the hustle of modern living, the game combines relaxing life simulation mechanics with persistent online multiplayer and character progression.

At the core of the experience is your personal homestead—an individual parcel of land known as your Crossroads. While you have complete creative freedom to cultivate and decorate your private space, the world does not isolate you. At the center of every player's property stands a mysterious magical signpost. Interacting with this ancient marker lets you venture directly into the realms of friends and fellow wanderers in seamless online co-op.

The game embraces what the developers affectionately describe as "cozy chaos"—gentle, dynamic random world events, seasonal surprises, and organic group interactions that keep daily routines lively without injecting punishing difficulty or high-stakes stress.

## How to Join the Little Crossroads Beta and Public Playtest

Red Merle Games has initiated public playtest phases via Steam to stress-test their networking systems and gather community feedback on pacing and skill mechanics. Joining the **little crossroads beta** does not require complicated third-party signups or Discord verification lotteries. Everything is handled directly through Valve's built-in platform features.

To register your interest and request participation:

1. Launch your Steam desktop client or open your browser.
2. Navigate directly to the official [Steam store page for Little Crossroads](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3627770/Little_Crossroads/).
3. Log into your active Steam account.
4. Locate the section titled **Join the Little Crossroads Playtest**.
5. Click the green **Request Access** button.
6. Check your Steam notifications and linked email address regularly; Steam will notify you instantly when the developer rolls out the next invitation batch.

The playtest uses rolling waves, meaning developers admit cohorts of testers based on server capacity and targeted milestone feedback.

## Core Gameplay Systems: Progression, Gathering, and Companions

Unlike traditional farming sims that constrain your daily activities with a rigid, punishing stamina bar or severe fainting penalties, *Little Crossroads* focuses on open-ended player autonomy. You can spend twelve continuous in-game hours by the riverside reeling in rare aquatic creatures, or you can spend your day scaling cliff sides to uncover mineral nodes.

### Gathering and Life Skills

The early builds highlight five foundational professions that dictate how you interact with nature:

*   **Fishing:** Cast into babbling brooks, mountain ponds, and tidal pools with distinct rod tiers and mini-games tuned for relaxing reflex play.
*   **Mining:** Break open shimmering geodes and veins buried deep within underground caverns to extract gemstones and structural ore.
*   **Bug Catching:** Track rare insects across meadows and sun-dappled forests using specialized nets.
*   **Woodcutting:** Harvest various timber types to supply your personal workshop with materials for fences, benches, and homestead extensions.
*   **Foraging:** Gather native flora, wild seeds, and herbal components used in crafting teas, dishes, and dyes.

| Skill Discipline | Primary Gathering Tool | Key Material Yields | Primary Utility in Homestead |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fishing** | Simple to Master Rods | Fresh fish, river treasure, pearls | Cooking recipes, aquariums, trade |
| **Mining** | Pickaxes, Heavy Hammers | Iron ore, raw crystals, stone slabs | Structural crafting, tool forge upgrades |
| **Bug Catching** | Silk and Mesh Nets | Butterflies, beetles, glow-bugs | Terrarium displays, dye pigments |
| **Woodcutting** | Axes, Logging Saws | Softwood, aged hardwood, tree sap | Furniture fabrication, framing |
| **Foraging** | Hand Gathering, Trowels | Wild mushrooms, herbs, wild seeds | Gardening starter kits, meal buffs |

### Groundbreaking Pet Utility: Meet the Grolem

One of the most notable features highlighted during early developer streams is the inclusion of functional companions. Foremost among them is the "Grolem"—a lovable, ambulatory dirt golem. 

The Grolem is not merely a cosmetic vanity follower; it functions as a walking miniature farm plot. By planting crop seeds directly into the Grolem's fertile back, your crops water and nurture themselves passively while you wander across the wilderness. As you fish, explore, or socialize in multiplayer hubs, your companion steadily tends your harvest, eliminating the repetitive chore of running back to static soil plots.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I get access to the little crossroads beta?
You can request access directly through the game's official Steam store page by clicking the "Request Access" button in the Playtest section. When the development team rolls out new participant slots, you will receive an automatic email and Steam notification granting download permissions.

### When is the full version of Little Crossroads releasing?
Red Merle Games has scheduled the official release of *Little Crossroads* for 2027. Ahead of launch, multiple testing phases, including the **little crossroads beta** and open playtest weekends, will take place to polish features and stabilize multiplayer infrastructure.

### Does the game support single-player play?
Yes. Although the game is built with real-time online co-op in mind, you are completely free to treat your Crossroads as a solitary retreat. You can spend your entire time fishing, mining, crafting, and gardening solo without opening your signpost to outside visitors.

### Can I play the playtest on Steam Deck?
Official Steam Deck compatibility remains unrated by Valve at this stage of development. However, because the minimum hardware requirements are modest and standard Xbox controller support is integrated into the client, player experience reports indicate the game can run under Valve's Proton compatibility layer with minimal configuration tweaks.
